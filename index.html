About

Designing for humans in complex systems

I'm a product designer with deep experience turning ambiguous enterprise challenges into clear, human-centered digital experiences. I've shipped product across consumer tech, enterprise SaaS, healthcare, energy, and financial services.

At Meta, I worked on SaaS tools used by sales teams worldwide — from internal CRMs to marketing platforms for small businesses. Before that, I drove research-led design transformations for clients including State Farm, Florida Power & Light, and Texas Health.

I believe great design is less about aesthetics and more about clarity under constraints — and I thrive at the intersection of strategy, research, and craft.
